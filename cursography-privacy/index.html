Privacy Policy — Cursography

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Cursography (the “App”) is a handwriting practice app for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes the App’s privacy practices. It is short because the App is built to work entirely offline and to collect nothing.

Data collection

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required, and none is offered.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection tools.
  • The App makes no network connections for its features. Every lesson works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your practice progress, settings, and any practice sheets (PDFs) you generate are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. PDFs are created on the device and go only where you choose to send or print them. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Support contact

If you contact us for support by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message and use them only to respond to you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

The App is designed for adults learning or improving handwriting. It collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
