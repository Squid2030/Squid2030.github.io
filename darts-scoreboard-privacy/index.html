Privacy Policy — Darts Scoreboard Plus

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Darts Scoreboard Plus is a darts scoring app that works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features.

Data stored on your device

Player names, match history and career statistics you enter are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.

Support contact

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to reply. Support: https://www.guidehunting.com/privacy

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, the change will be described here before it ships.
