Ghost Box Plus — Support

No sound?

Open the Help tab in the app and run the sound check. Check the silent switch and volume, and try the phone speaker rather than headphones first.

The microphone prompt

Recording the room is on by default, so the first time you start a session the app asks for microphone access. If you tap Don't Allow, sessions still run and record the box only. To change your answer later: Settings > Ghost Box Plus > Microphone.

What the app can and can't do

Ghost Box Plus is a hobby tool. It makes no scientific claims: the sounds and words are generated by the app, and the meter shows your phone's real magnetic sensor. The Help tab explains every term where you first meet it.
