Privacy Policy — TurretTrue

Last updated: October 20, 2026

TurretTrue is a rifle ballistics calculator that runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data, sends no data anywhere, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your rifles, loads, settings, and truing results are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never transmitted. If you use the app’s Export backup feature, the backup file goes only where you send it (for example, your own Files or email) — the app does not receive, see, or keep a copy anywhere else.

Sensors

If you choose to use them, the app can read your device’s barometer and location on the device only to fill in air pressure, altitude, latitude, and shooting direction for its calculations. Those readings are used in the moment, are not stored as a location history, and are never transmitted.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes this position, the change will be described here and in the app’s App Store privacy label before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on the app’s App Store page.
