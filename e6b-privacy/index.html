Privacy Policy — E6B Flight Computer Plus

Last updated: October 31, 2026

E6B Flight Computer Plus is a flight-planning calculator that runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data, sends no data anywhere, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections at all.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all — no location, no camera, no contacts, no notifications. Every calculation happens on your device with the numbers you type in.

Data stored on your device

None. The app has no accounts and saves nothing between launches — each calculation starts fresh from the numbers on screen.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes this position, the change will be described here and in the app’s App Store privacy label before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on the app’s App Store page.
