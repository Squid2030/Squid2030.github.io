Privacy Policy — Ghost Box Plus

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Ghost Box Plus is a spirit box, magnetic meter and word tool for ghost hunting and ITC (instrumental trans-communication) practice. It works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party services and no network features at all. It never connects to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Session recordings, session notes and titles, word transcripts, your custom word list and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere. The only way a recording leaves your phone is if you choose to share it yourself with the share button.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so a session can record the room as well as the box. Recordings are your files, kept on your device. If you decline microphone access, the app still works and records the box only.

Motion and magnetic sensor

The meter reads your device's built-in magnetic sensor on the device itself. No sensor data is stored anywhere except the readings written into a session you record, on your device.
