Privacy Policy — Air Force Publications Plus

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Air Force Publications Plus is a library of publicly releasable U.S. Air Force publications that runs on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data. It has no user accounts and contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Network use

The app makes exactly one kind of network request: when you open a publication, it downloads that publication’s PDF directly from the official Air Force e-Publishing website (www.e-publishing.af.mil), a U.S. government website. That is an ordinary web request — the app sends no information about you, your device, or your reading to us or to anyone else. Like any website visit, the government server sees the request in its own server logs. Downloaded publications are stored on your device so you can read them offline.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions — no location, no camera, no contacts, no notifications.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, recently opened publications, and downloaded PDFs are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changed this policy, the updated policy would be posted on this page before that version shipped.
