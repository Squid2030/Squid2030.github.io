Privacy Policy — My Construction Pal

Last updated: October 31, 2026

My Construction Pal is a construction calculator that runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data, sends no data anywhere, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections at all.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and your calculation tape (history) are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never transmitted. If you use the app’s Share feature to send your tape as text, that text goes only where you send it (for example, your own email or messages) — the app does not receive, see, or keep a copy anywhere else.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes this position, the change will be described here and in the app’s App Store privacy label before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on the app’s App Store page.
