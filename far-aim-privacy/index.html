Privacy Policy — FAR/AIM Plus

Last updated: October 31, 2026

FAR/AIM Plus is an offline reference for aviation regulations that runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data and sends no data anywhere. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests at all.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions — no location, no camera, no contacts, no notifications.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, and notes are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changed this policy, the updated policy would be posted on this page before that version shipped. (One named future possibility: an optional in-app content refresh would download updated regulation files from a static host — an anonymous file fetch with no account and no tracking. If that feature ever ships, this policy will describe it first.)
